Fee Ledger Audit

The fee system is honest about one bill at a time and silent about the student. It has no idea what a family owes, only what each challan owes — and that single gap is behind every question you asked.

As of 20 October 2026 Read from schema, triggers, 580 lines of actions, 7 screens Live data 1 challan · 1 payment · 1 policy · 3 students

How it works today

Three tables carry the money. A fee policy says what a class owes in which months — tuition, admission, exam, transport, other, each with its own month list. A challan is one bill: one student, one month, one fee type, and a unique index enforces exactly that. A payment is money against exactly one challan.

Two triggers do the arithmetic. On every challan, net = amount + late_fee − discount. On every payment, the challan's paid_amount is re-summed and its status set: paid if covered, partial if some, overdue if past due, else open. Nothing in the app writes those two columns by hand, which is the right design.

What does not exist, anywhere: an account. No running balance, no credit, no "brought forward". Every number on every screen is a sum over challans computed at render time — the student page adds up billed − collected, the reports call fee_totals(), which does the same thing over the whole school. The screen called Student Ledger is a list of challans beside a list of payments with three totals above them. It is a derivation, not a ledger.

Two things are in the schema and used by nothing. fee_items and fee_challan_items — a price list and line items per challan — appear only in the backup route. And the student record carries fee_plan (monthly / quarterly / annual) and starting_month, but the monthly generator bills every active student every policy month regardless. A quarterly student is billed monthly.

The design that fixes it

Add one table and change the meaning of two. Keep every existing screen working during the change — the live data is one challan and one payment, so there is nothing to migrate, only a shape to settle.

1 · The ledger is the truth

fee_ledger: one row per thing that happened to a student's money — a charge when a challan is issued, a payment when cash arrives, a late fee, a discount, an advance, an adjustment. Signed amounts, dated, pointing back to the challan or payment that caused them. Balance is the sum; a trigger keeps students.fee_balance current so it is one column read, not a query. Positive means owed, negative means in credit.

That one sheet answers every question above. Five unpaid months are five debit lines and a balance; the Rs 30,000 clears them and leaves Rs 5,975 in credit; November's challan is issued and immediately absorbed. The parent never saw a refused payment and the cashier never did arithmetic.

2 · A challan is a header with lines

Keep fee_challans as the voucher — the number, the due date, the student, the totals — and give it period_from / period_to. Put the months in fee_challan_items, the table that already exists: one line per month per component. Move the one-per-month rule from the header to the lines: a line for Ahmed · November · tuition may exist once. Then a five-month advance voucher is five lines on one challan, and when the monthly batch runs in December it finds December already billed and skips him. Nothing duplicates.

Three more header columns make the voucher honest: previous_balance (the account balance at issue), credit_applied (what was absorbed from credit), and payable — what the parent actually takes to the bank.

Three copies — bank, school, student — is the convention every Pakistani parent recognises, and it is what the print view should produce: the same voucher three times down an A4 sheet with cut lines, not a web page screenshot.

3 · A payment is against the account, allocated to challans

Make fee_payments.challan_id optional and add fee_payment_allocations: payment, challan, amount. The cashier picks a student, sees every open challan and the current credit, types the total handed over. The system allocates oldest-first — June before July — shows the split, and lets the cashier override it. Whatever is left over becomes a credit entry in the ledger. One receipt, listing what it cleared and what it put on account.

The existing recalc trigger survives almost unchanged: it sums allocations instead of payments, so partial and paid still set themselves.

4 · Generation that knows the student

Move generateMonthlyBatch into a SQL function. For each student it reads fee_plan: monthly students get a one-line challan, quarterly students get three lines on one voucher every third month from starting_month, annual students one voucher a year. It writes the previous_balance from the ledger, applies credit, computes payable, and writes the ledger entries in the same transaction. Being SQL, a scheduled job can call it on the 1st with no button and no person.

In this order, because each step leaves every screen working. Sizes are honest for a module with one live challan.

What was built, screen by screen

Collect Fee now starts from the student. Pick a family and the panel lists every bill they have open — not only the month on screen, because arrears are the point — alongside their balance and any advance they hold. One amount is typed; the split across bills is shown before anything is saved, oldest first, with any surplus named as advance. The cashier can override the split per bill. One payment, one receipt, however many months it settles.

Bill Ahead sits on the student's ledger: choose a starting month and a number of months, and one voucher is raised covering them all, with one line per month off the class fee policy. Months already billed are left out, so the monthly run and an advance voucher cannot bill the same month twice — that rule is a unique index, not a check in the app.

The voucher prints three copies to an A4 sheet as before, but now lists its months as separate lines and shows what the family was carrying when it was raised, any advance spent against it, and the figure actually payable. The receipt lists every bill the payment cleared, and the balance printed on it is read off the ledger as at that receipt — so a reprint never contradicts the copy the parent kept.

Student Ledger is a statement: every charge and payment in the order it happened, with the running balance after each and an "as at" date for answering what was owed in March. A negative balance is shown as credit, not as a debt.

Late fees can now be charged per day past the grace period or as a flat amount per month overdue, and either can be capped per challan. On a five-month arrear an uncapped daily fine comes to more than the fee itself, which is why the cap exists.

The monthly run can go unattended. Point any scheduler at POST /api/cron/fees once a day with the token in an Authorization: Bearer header; each school names its own billing day and due day in Settings → Fee & Finance, and the database decides whether today is that day. Mint the token by calling rotate_fee_cron_token() as a platform admin — it is returned once and only its hash is stored. Re-running a day that already ran raises nothing, so retries are safe. It ships switched off: the thing being automated writes bills for every family in the school, and a school that has not yet set its billing day and its fee policies would find out from parents.

Decisions you need to make first

Four choices the code cannot make for you. Each has a recommendation; none is blocking the first two migrations.

These four were live questions when this was written. Three are now settled in code, and the fourth is a setting. Each note below says what was chosen and where to change it.

Late fees on arrears: per month, or once?

Today five overdue months earn five separate per-day fines — Rs 1,525 in the example, growing daily. Most schools charge a flat late fee per missed month, or one fine on the account, not a per-day meter on each. Per-day across five challans is unusually steep and will generate complaints.

Recommend: flat amount per overdue month, capped, set in Settings → Fee & Finance.

Settled: the recommendation was taken, and both modes exist. Settings → Fee & Finance now offers so much per day late or a flat amount per month late, with a maximum fine per challan that applies to either. Nothing is charged until somebody runs the late-fee pass from the collection screen, and the fine is recomputed from the due date on each run rather than added to, so running it twice in a day does not double it.

Credit: absorbed automatically, or on request?

Automatic means November's voucher already shows credit applied and the parent pays less. On request means the credit sits until the cashier applies it. Automatic is what a parent expects when they paid ahead.

Recommend: automatic at issue, visible as a line on the voucher.

Settled: automatic, as recommended. raise_fee_voucher() calls apply_credit() as its last step, so a family in credit gets a voucher that already shows less: advance applied and a reduced figure to pay. The advance is spent oldest payment first, and the Collect Fee panel also stops suggesting money a family does not owe — it suggests the bills less the credit, so nobody is charged twice for the same month.

Arrears: on the current voucher, or a separate one?

One voucher with a "previous balance" line is what gets paid. A separate arrears voucher is cleaner for accounting but gets ignored at the bank counter.

Recommend: one voucher, previous balance as its own line, arrears paid first by allocation.

Settled: one voucher, as recommended. Every challan stores the balance the family was carrying the moment it was raised, in previous_balance, and prints it as arrears brought forward above the total. The figure is stamped at issue time rather than computed at print time, so a voucher reprinted in December still shows what December's voucher showed. Payments then clear the oldest bill first, which is the other half of "arrears paid first".

Advance vouchers: who may raise them?

A five-month voucher is a request, not a schedule. Raising one should be a deliberate act on the student's page — "Bill ahead · Nov to Mar" — with the ordinary batch then skipping those months automatically.

Recommend: an explicit action on the student page, available to anyone with fees edit.
